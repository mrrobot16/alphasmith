# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to alphalocksmithngarage.com looking for locksmith, garage door, automatic gate, fencing or access-control service in the Portland, OR area. *Inferred from the mockup and not confirmed by the owner:* most arrive on a phone, often urgent (lockout, broken spring, stuck gate), and want to reach a real person quickly. A secondary audience of homeowners and businesses with planned work (installs, access control) is likely; confirm before designing for it.

## Product Purpose

A lead-generation website for Alpha Locksmith & Garage. Success is a phone call or a submitted quote request, so calls to action must be strong and always reachable (requirements.md item 1). The site must also be SEO-ready and instrumented with Google Analytics and Google Ads (items 3 to 5).

## Positioning

Owner-run: Andres owns and runs Alpha, and callers talk to the team that does the work (confirmed by the owner). Open 24/7, licensed Oregon contractor and certified locksmith.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: locksmith, garage / overhead doors, automatic gates, fencing, access control.
- Service area: a 30-mile radius around Portland, OR; city pages (e.g. Beaverton) and service pages (e.g. garage door spring repair) share one template.
- Primary action: call 503-505-8560. Secondary: the quote request form.
- Fully mobile friendly (requirements.md item 2).
- SEO-ready structure only. Content creation is explicitly out of scope (item 3); copy placeholders in brackets stay until the owner supplies real content.
- Undecided: where quote requests are delivered (email or CRM); real logo and photo files; Google Ads and Analytics account IDs.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Alpha Locksmith & Garage. Existing logo set (primary, wordmark, stacked, icon mark; mascot in primary) lives in the design canvas and still needs exporting to the project.

## Evidence on Hand

- Contractor license CCB#248737; locksmith certification 62990CLS; BBB rating A+; insured and bonded. Use these exactly as written.
- Not available yet, so do not fabricate: customer reviews, prices, warranty terms, years in business beyond the "10+ years" in the mockup (unconfirmed), neighborhood lists, job photos.

## Product Principles

1. The phone number is the product: every screen is one tap from a call.
2. Show the real people and credentials; never invent proof.
3. Mobile-first, because urgent visitors are on phones.
4. Structure pages for search (one clear topic per page, local landing pages) without padding content.
5. Keep placeholders honest and visibly bracketed until real content exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement was stated. Baseline: readable text sizes, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support.
